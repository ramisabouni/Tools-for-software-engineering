# Measurement and figure review checklist

## Before plotting

- State the question, claim, audience, experimental unit, and field contract.
- Validate schema, types, units, allowed categories, ranges, observation-key
  uniqueness, planned coverage, and repetition counts.
- Reconcile raw = accepted + rejected and accepted = plotted observations.
- Preserve collection order, repeated measurements, raw data, and rejection reasons.
- Investigate extremes; apply only predeclared, documented exclusions.

## Statistics and visual argument

- Match summary and uncertainty to the question and assumptions.
- Show individual observations or distribution shape when practical.
- State sample size and define every interval or error bar.
- Use honest baselines and scales; do not infer causation from association.

## Accessibility and publication

- Label quantities, units, transformations, and logarithmic scales.
- Combine accessible color with markers, line styles, shapes, or direct labels.
- Inspect grayscale, final dimensions, reading order, and alternative text.
- Make the caption identify system revision, workload, statistic, uncertainty,
  sample size, units, and principal limitation.

## Reproducibility

- Save raw and analysis data, source, parameters, command, tool versions, and
  input/output digests.
- Regenerate from a clean location and prevent stale outputs surviving failures.
